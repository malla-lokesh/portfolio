In February, Skailama took the whole team to Thailand for a few days — a company offsite, funded end to end. Most of our time was in Pattaya, not Bangkok itself. These are a few photos from those days.

### 12 February

We started late morning with brunch at Harudot Chonburi by Nana Coffee Roasters, then headed to The Bayview Hotel. In the evening we had dinner along Pattaya Beach and walked the night view for a bit.
